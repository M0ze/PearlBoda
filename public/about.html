Our Story

Moving Uganda Forward, One Ride at a Time

PearlBoda was born from a simple observation: millions of Ugandans rely on boda bodas every day, yet finding a safe, reliable ride remains a challenge. We set out to change that.

Our Mission

Empowering Local Transport

We believe every Ugandan deserves access to safe, affordable, and reliable transportation. PearlBoda connects passengers with verified boda riders across Hioma, Kampala, and Fort Portal — creating opportunities for drivers while serving communities.

Our platform is built with local needs in mind: from transparent pricing in Ugandan shillings to support for areas where traditional ride-hailing doesn't reach.

"Transportation is not just about getting from A to B — it's about connecting people, enabling commerce, and building stronger communities."
